Privacy policy

Welcome to Fact Drawer. Please read this short privacy notice before using the app.

  • Fact Drawer uses the information you submit to provide its features, such as publishing facts, saving preferences, and managing your account.
  • Some settings or activity may be stored on this device. If the app is connected to its backend, submitted facts and related moderation data may be sent to that service.
  • Links to sources are submitted by users. Check links carefully before opening them.

Do not enter any personal information or identifiers. Do not include private details in usernames, fact titles, explanations, or source submissions.
